# ADR-0004: Skill naming, prefixing, and size conventions

## Status

Accepted (2026-06-01)

## Context

With a master/mother/child structure in a flat skill namespace, naming has to do two jobs: make routing legible to humans and avoid description collisions for Claude. Anthropic guidance caps `name` at 64 chars (lowercase/hyphen) and `description` at 1024 chars, recommends third-person "what + when" descriptions, and recommends keeping the SKILL.md body under 500 lines ([Skill authoring best practices](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/agents-and-tools/agent-skills/best-practices)).

## Decision

- **Child skills are prefixed by their mother:** `stack-*`, `ai-*`, `data-*`, `infra-*`, `security-*`, `quality-*`, `legal-*` (mother `domain-legal`), `meta-*` (mother `project-meta`), `workflow-*`. Directory name = `name:` frontmatter exactly.
- **Mothers are brief index skills** (<100 lines): trigger, child routing table, one-screen defaults, banned anti-patterns, relationships.
- **Children stay under 500 lines.** Skills exceeding it move detail into a one-level-deep `references/` subdir and keep SKILL.md as the navigable overview. Current over-cap candidates: `infra-s3-uploads` (552), `infra-background-jobs` (548), `stack-typescript-strict` (526).
- **Descriptions are third-person, lead with what the skill does, then "Use when…", and end with trigger keywords.** This is the only routing signal, so mothers own broad triggers and children use narrow, distinctive terms.
- **A versioned `skills.json`** (generated by `scripts/build-registry.sh`) records name/mother/lines/description for every skill and is regenerated on change.

## Consequences

- `git mv`-renaming the 29 existing children is a one-time breaking change; cross-references in skill bodies, README, and CLAUDE.md were rewritten in the same pass, and frontmatter `name:` synced to the new directory.
- Prefixing groups skills on the filesystem and in the registry without affecting routing (which is description-based).
- The 500-line trims are tracked as follow-up; they don't block the restructure.
